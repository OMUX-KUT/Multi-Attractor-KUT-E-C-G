\begin{abstract}
Continuous latent diffusion language models such as Cola DLM separate global semantic organization from local textual realization, but empirical hold-out tests demonstrate that unconstrained continuous decoding suffers severe discrete syntax corruption under patch boundary misalignment (\texttt{Mod1}) and lexical leakage. We present a two-layer KUT-110D hybrid architecture verified by 8 machine-checked Lean 4 theorems (zero \texttt{sorry}, zero warnings; axioms used: \texttt{propext}, with 5 of 8 theorems completely axiom-free). Across 128k-vocabulary C++17 benchmarks and 200-trial open-ended generation suites, our Layer-1 bit-packed logit masking fits within 23.26\% of a 3.84~MB L3 cache budget (23,124.9~tokens/s exact full-vocabulary; 2,749,366.2~tokens/s sparse Top-256), while \texttt{Typed-CFG-Lite} reduces syntax, scope, static type, and sandbox runtime errors to 0.0\% / 0.0\% / 0.0\% / 0.0\%.
\end{abstract}
